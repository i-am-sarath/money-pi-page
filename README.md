# Money Pi — legal pages

Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions for the Money Pi app, served with GitHub Pages.

- `/privacy/` — Privacy Policy (iPhone and Android)
- `/terms/` — Terms & Conditions (iPhone and Android, including the Android-only Money Pi Pro subscription)
- `/ios/privacy/` — Privacy Policy for the iPhone/iPad app only
- `/ios/terms/` — Terms of Use for the iPhone/iPad app only (no purchases, no subscription)

The iOS app links to the `/ios/` pages. Use those URLs in App Store Connect too.
