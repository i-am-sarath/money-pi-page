Privacy Policy

Money Pi — AI-Powered Budget Tracker  ·  Published by Sarath Ramesh  ·  Last updated: October 28, 2026

Who We Are

Money Pi ("the App", "we", "us") is an AI-powered personal budget tracker for iPhone and Android. The App is developed and published by Sarath Ramesh, an individual developer. All references to "we" in this policy refer to Sarath Ramesh.

The short version: Your personal budget data stays on your device. We use your microphone only when you log an expense by voice. An account is optional and is only needed for voice logging and shared Groups. We do not sell your personal data and we do not track you across other apps or websites. Money Pi has no ads.

Permissions We Request

  • Microphone (iPhone and Android) — Used only while you hold the voice button to log an expense. The recording is sent to our server for transcription and is not stored by us.
  • Speech Recognition (iPhone) — Used to turn what you say into an expense entry. Speech recognition on iPhone may be performed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Notification access (Android, optional) — If you turn on auto-detect, the App reads bank and UPI payment notifications to suggest transactions. They are read and processed on your device and are not uploaded to us.
  • Display over other apps and notifications (Android, optional) — Used for the quick-log bubble and for transaction review reminders.

The App does not request access to your camera, photos, contacts, or location.

Data We Collect

You can use manual expense tracking without an account. We collect the following only when you use the related feature:

  • Account details — If you sign in (Sign in with Apple on iPhone, Google on Android, or an email code on either), we store your email address and a user ID. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to share your name, we store it as your display name. If you choose Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive only the relay address.
  • Voice audio and transcripts — When you log by voice, the recording and the resulting text are sent through our server to our AI provider to be transcribed and turned into an expense entry. We do not keep the audio or the transcript on our servers.
  • Voice usage count — We keep a monthly count of voice entries per user ID to apply the fair-use limit.
  • Shared Groups data — If you use Groups, the shared expenses (amount, category, note, who paid, how it is split), group names, and members' display names are stored in our cloud database so that group members can see them.
  • Diagnostics — Crash and error reports that help us fix bugs. They do not include your financial records.
  • Purchase status (Android only) — Whether you have an active Money Pi Pro subscription. We never receive your card, UPI, or bank details.

All of the above is used to provide the App's features. None of it is used for advertising or tracking.

Data That Stays on Your Device

Your personal transactions, accounts, budgets, categories, trips, recurring rules, and settings are stored only on your device, including the App's local backup file. We cannot see this data. Deleting the App removes it.

On Android you can optionally back up to a Google Sheet in your own Google Drive. The App can only access the sheet it creates or that you pick, and that data is held by Google under your Google account, not by us.

Ads and Tracking

Money Pi contains no ads and no advertising SDKs on any platform, does not access your device's advertising identifier, and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Subscriptions and Payments

iPhone: The iPhone app currently has no subscription and no in-app purchases; all features are included.

Android: Money Pi Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription sold and billed entirely through Google Play Billing. Billing, renewals, and cancellations are managed in your Google Play account.

Third-Party Services

We use a limited set of service providers, each only for its stated purpose:

  • Supabase — Account sign-in and the Groups database. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare — Hosts our server that relays voice requests and keeps the usage count. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Groq — AI provider that transcribes voice audio and extracts the expense details. See Groq's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple and speech recognition on iPhone. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Sentry — Crash and error diagnostics. See Sentry's Privacy Policy.
  • Google (Android only) — Google sign-in, Google Sheets backup, and Google Play Billing. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat (Android only) — Manages subscription status. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal or financial data, and we do not share it with data brokers.

Data Retention & Deleting Your Account

Voice audio is processed only for the duration of the request. Account and Groups data is kept until you delete your account.

You can delete your account at any time inside the App: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and the cloud data linked to it. You can also email us at the address below and we will delete it for you.

We recommend keeping your device and the App updated to benefit from the latest security improvements.

Children's Privacy

Money Pi is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
